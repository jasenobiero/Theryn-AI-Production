---
name: THERYN interface direction
description: The product-level visual direction for THERYN across web and mobile.
---

THERYN should combine the strongest interaction patterns from ChatGPT and Gemini while keeping its own red-on-black identity: spacious and focused on desktop, native and touch-first on mobile, with shared chat, history, navigation, and settings language.

**Why:** The user wants one recognizable THERYN experience across desktop and mobile rather than separate platform designs.

**How to apply:** Preserve the existing product branding and routes, favor focused conversation flows, compact model/context controls, accessible history, and responsive behavior over copying either competitor's branding.
